Mathematics provides a language for understanding complexity, structure and change.

I am an Assistant Professor of Mathematics in the School of Sciences at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), India. My research specializes in continuous wavelet analysis, particularly the Mexican Hat Wavelet Transform, Wavelet–Stieltjes transforms, generalized functions, distributions, ultradistributions and Boehmian spaces. I completed my PhD in Mathematics at Banasthali Vidyapith in 2022.

My theoretical work addresses representation, inversion, reconstruction, localization and asymptotic properties of integral transforms, with a particular interest in Abelian and Tauberian analysis. My current research connects mathematically rigorous multiscale approaches with time–frequency analysis, partial differential equations, physiological signal analysis and structural-damage localization.

With more than four years of university-level teaching experience, I teach mathematics across science, engineering and data science programmes. I emphasize mathematical rigour, visualization, problem-based learning and appropriate computational tools. Alongside teaching, I mentor undergraduate researchers, guide bachelor's students in research projects, and contribute to academic leadership, curriculum development and scientific outreach.
